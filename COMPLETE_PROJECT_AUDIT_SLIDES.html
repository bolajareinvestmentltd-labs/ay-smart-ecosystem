🔐 Trust & Financial System

The Money-Safe Foundation

Protects buyers, sellers, and the platform through verified identity, secure escrow holds, and immutable audit trails.

1. KYC Verification

Users submit NIN, selfie, and documents. Third-party provider (Dojah) verifies identity with 95%+ accuracy.

2. Wallet System

Each user has a wallet. Only verified users can transact. Balance is real-time and audited.

3. Escrow Holds

Buyer funds are debited to escrow, not released. Admin reviews and either releases to seller or refunds buyer.

4. Audit Trail

Every transaction creates immutable log: who, what, when, why. Stored forever for compliance and disputes.

5. Admin Review

All escrow decisions require admin approval. Can release, refund, or escalate disputes.

6. Financial Audit

Admin views reconciliation: total volume, held escrow, processed transactions, wallet balances.

2. User Responsibilities

  • KYC verification is mandatory for all transaction activities.

  • Users must comply with all applicable laws and regulations in their jurisdiction.

3. KYC & Verification Requirements

  • All users must complete KYC verification with valid government-issued ID.
  • Rejected applications can be resubmitted with corrected information.
  • Admin approval is required after KYC verification completion.
  • False information in KYC can result in permanent account suspension.

📋 Terms & Conditions (Part 2)

4. Financial & Escrow Terms

  • All transactions are held in secure escrow until completion.
  • Buyer funds are debited from wallet and held in escrow, not released directly to sellers.
  • Admin review and approval required for all escrow releases.
  • Refunds are processed within 5-7 business days to original payment method.
  • Transaction fees are non-refundable and disclosed before checkout.

5. Dispute Resolution

  • Disputes must be reported within 7 days of transaction completion.
  • Platform admins will investigate and make binding decisions.
  • Evidence (images, receipts, messages) should be provided for investigation.
  • Fraudulent disputes can result in account suspension and legal action.

6. Privacy & Data Protection

  • User data is encrypted and stored securely in compliance with GDPR and NDPR.
  • KYC documents are retained for regulatory compliance and audit purposes.
  • Personal information is never shared with third parties without consent, except for payment processing.
  • Users have the right to request data deletion within legal limitations.
